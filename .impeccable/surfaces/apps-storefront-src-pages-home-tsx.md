## Scope

The storefront of Sydvest Kaffebrenneri (`apps/storefront`). The home page is Persuade; shop, product, cart, checkout, pay and order pages are Operate and inherit the world with restraint. Code-led. The chosen sketch is `.impeccable/mocks/decision/model-pick.html`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Oilskin yellow is the shop's coat: it carries the brand and every primary action through Bergen's rain. Refuses the roaster default of kraft bags, stamps and cream paper, and the neutral grid with a big bag photo.

OWN-WORLD: Wet slate #11161b ground, raised wet slate #1a2128 surfaces, chalk text #e9e6df, mist #a7afb7; oilskin yellow #f3c623 as a material field with oil-ink #1b1608 on it; warm window amber #e8a04a only for "inside, warm": the counter, the roast scale, open hours. A faint slanted rain streak on the ground. Square corners, one curved oilskin edge. Schibsted Grotesk, tabular figures. Light theme: overcast fog grey with slate ink, the yellow kept as the coat, darkened where it carries text.

STORY: A visitor on a dark evening sees a roaster from Bergen, the beans and the gear with honest prices in their currency, adds a bag, checks out as a guest and follows the order from a link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a 640px oilskin-yellow panel with a curved top-right edge: the headline "Rain all week. Coffee all week." at ~96px, one line on Morning roast, and the working Add button with its price. Right, on wet slate, the beans as cards with a seven-step light-to-dark roast scale, each with its Add; Decaf as "Soon". At the foot, an amber line: the counter's hours, closed Sundays. Header: the Sydvest wordmark, Beans, Gear, The counter, the country and currency, and the bag as a yellow chip.

FORM: grounded pick, first of seven on the ordered list (Sydvest oilskin); seed:717586a16ec9be5f. Signature interaction: a quiet rain canvas across the home page's first viewport, falling at a slant and still under prefers-reduced-motion; the roast scale fills from light to dark on reveal.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
