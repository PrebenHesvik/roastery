## The allocator (order of satisfaction)

1. **Fixed rows**: the script's steps verbatim; no generated step at a script moment or naming a cast id. The setup roasts are replaced by the stock plan.
2. **Lesson orders (600)** from `order_values`: country by `i % 10`; consumer unless bags ≥ 10, i = 600 or one of the 13 (V6); placed 10:00–15:00 UTC; lines bags × coffee at list price (+P-20 when `i % 11 == 0` on the web). Consumer paid → Pay a minute later; consumer unpaid → Invoice, open on 30 Sep; business → Settle by 30 Sep when paid. Ship on `order_date + 1` 09:00, Deliver per `deliveries` (297 takes 21 days). Order 300: Credit of a NO consumer's earlier NOK P-20 line on 18 May 2026, then Refund (W16).
3. **Subscribers (240)** by generated consumers: Subscribe 10:00 on `started`, Cancel 12:00 on `ended`; about one in six Skips (on the 1st) or Pause/Resume; ShipBags on the 3rd of each month, 06:00.
4. **Business accounts**: 8, two per country; the Bergen café takes order 600; postal codes on all.
5. **Filler demand** = target − lesson consumer bags − C-01's year, per day and product; feasibility refuses a negative; 1–2-bag web orders by a pool of about 1,500 consumers, 09:00–16:00 UTC, paid, shipped next day. Jan–Sep 2024 and 10–31 Oct 2026 use `targets.demand`. The panel's reached-4 anchors are filled first.
6. **Finland**: 2026-W17 (20–26 Apr), Finnish consumer filler cut to about a quarter; feasibility refuses a surrounding mean under 12.
7. **Visits (3,600)** direct-free panel with referrers, no variant; reached 4 → the anchor's order 7 min later. **Checkout test (4,000)**: direct, anonymous, 2 Mar–10 Apr 2026, variant by parity.
8. **Outside series**: weather and door counts from `shop_days`, extended to 31 Oct; old-till totals to 5 Oct (D12); fx every banking day with the script's 8 rows and the 2–7 Oct gaps; walk-in receipts 10–31 Oct.
9. **Stock**: Monday roasts sized from the generated bags plus the running subscriptions; BuyGreen quarterly ≥ kilos roasted; SetCost per product per year below price; occasional WriteOff; Stocktake P-10 42 and P-20 5 on 4 Oct 12:00.
10. **Jobs**: Relay 06:00/12:00/18:00/23:30 outside the window; CountAtClose every night; NightlyExtract and CsvExport every night of October only (the 9 Oct export is the script's and the faulted one).
