Place, Subscribe and Credit carry a handle (a plain `str`); dependents name a handle or a scripted id; `Played.handles` gives what each call returned; C-01's year uses handles (`_year_orders` gone; `hidden()` stays for the week's E-0000101..105). Twelve new actions, one contract-B call each; Pay and Settle carry their four fields, not a `PaymentMessage`; a Refund is `payments.refund`, its negative message a later Pay. `snapshots=` reads tables, feed, provider rows and the csv after the steps at each moment. Steps are read once with one step of look-ahead; equal moments are allowed, an earlier one refused. **R1 measured** (sqlite, 3 years × 30 orders a day, 137,513 steps): 391 s, every call linear in history, so the play is quadratic (≈ 40 s × years²). The outbox without an index on its batch and sent columns is 41 %; `orders.get` scanning `order_lines` by `order_id` most of the rest; `stock_movements(product_id)` about 10 %. All three are store-internal indexes (V15, widened).

## Context

6.4 froze contract B (`shop-0.1.0`) on the teaching week. 6.5 builds the world around it: two years
of volume (Jan 2024 to the week) as `full`, all of October 2026 as `month` with nightly landings,
and publishes `roastery-data-1.0.0`. Done when (plan §6.5): `test_roastery_tier3.py`'s literals
(ported: `packages/spec/tests/test_full_claims.py`) pass unchanged on `full`; the extracts test
passes (each tiny row in the extract its `lives_in` names); all 46 waiting claims pass; two machines
give the same sha256; `/code-review` has run; the release is published with its changelog, on the
owner's word.

**6.5 is a fit, not a free simulation.** `tier3.sql` (already in `roastery_spec/baseline`, sha in
PROVENANCE.toml; DuckDB 1.5.6 in both repos; integer-only, no randomness) gives eight target tables
with exact literals (daily_sales 1947 rows = 2,409,009.00; order_values mean 475.42; …). The
allocator builds a world, played through contract B, whose derivations (the 46 claims) equal them.

Findings that shaped the plan:
1. **Fit check (lead):** C-01's year fits under `daily_sales` everywhere; 13 lesson web orders
   exceed their day's P-11 quota. They become business accounts' orders (owner, 2026-11-05; V6).
2. **The feed cannot be quiet before the week** (lesson orders of 27–30 Sep deliver 2–5 Oct), but
   it needn't be: batches keep their rows and refusal is by content, so the invariant is "no
   generated Relay in [5 Oct 00:00, 10 Oct 07:00], no generated refusal" (V3).
3. **`test_extracts.py` L96–106** counts C-01's October orders and spend with no month filter;
   on the real `full` its October check would count the year's 20 against tiny's 3. Fixed at its
   subject in U0: both subqueries filter by the month.
4. **`variant` and `referrer` are disjoint**: test visitors are direct, panel visits carry no
   variant (V12).

## The allocator (order of satisfaction)

1. **Fixed rows**: the script's steps verbatim; no generated step at a script moment or naming a cast id. The setup roasts are replaced by the stock plan.
2. **Lesson orders (600)** from `order_values`: country by `i % 10`; consumer unless bags ≥ 10, i = 600 or one of the 13 (V6); placed 10:00–15:00 UTC; lines bags × coffee at list price (+P-20 when `i % 11 == 0` on the web). Consumer paid → Pay a minute later; consumer unpaid → Invoice, open on 30 Sep; business → Settle by 30 Sep when paid. Ship on `order_date + 1` 09:00, Deliver per `deliveries` (297 takes 21 days). Order 300: Credit of a NO consumer's earlier NOK P-20 line on 18 May 2026, then Refund (W16).
3. **Subscribers (240)** by generated consumers: Subscribe 10:00 on `started`, Cancel 12:00 on `ended`; about one in six Skips (on the 1st) or Pause/Resume; ShipBags on the 3rd of each month, 06:00.
4. **Business accounts**: 8, two per country; the Bergen café takes order 600; postal codes on all.
5. **Filler demand** = target − lesson consumer bags − C-01's year, per day and product; feasibility refuses a negative; 1–2-bag web orders by a pool of about 1,500 consumers, 09:00–16:00 UTC, paid, shipped next day. Jan–Sep 2024 and 10–31 Oct 2026 use `targets.demand`. The panel's reached-4 anchors are filled first.
6. **Finland**: 2026-W17 (20–26 Apr), Finnish consumer filler cut to about a quarter; feasibility refuses a surrounding mean under 12.
7. **Visits (3,600)** direct-free panel with referrers, no variant; reached 4 → the anchor's order 7 min later. **Checkout test (4,000)**: direct, anonymous, 2 Mar–10 Apr 2026, variant by parity.
8. **Outside series**: weather and door counts from `shop_days`, extended to 31 Oct; old-till totals to 5 Oct (D12); fx every banking day with the script's 8 rows and the 2–7 Oct gaps; walk-in receipts 10–31 Oct.
9. **Stock**: Monday roasts sized from the generated bags plus the running subscriptions; BuyGreen quarterly ≥ kilos roasted; SetCost per product per year below price; occasional WriteOff; Stocktake P-10 42 and P-20 5 on 4 Oct 12:00.
10. **Jobs**: Relay 06:00/12:00/18:00/23:30 outside the window; CountAtClose every night; NightlyExtract and CsvExport every night of October only (the 9 Oct export is the script's and the faulted one).
