## 1. Context

**Why this step comes first.** Every later step builds against the data contract:
- the app (6.3) must be able to write each app-made table;
- the simulator (6.4, 6.5) is tuned until the claims pass;
- data-docs (6.6) reads the tables by name, grain and key.

Today the contract is spread over `dataset.sql`'s headers, two test files, the master plan's
catalog and the phase plan. 6.1 gathers it into **one place that a test checks**.

**Outcome.**
- `packages/spec` holds the contract as code, a readable `contract.md` rendered from it, and every claim of data-docs' two test files, ported with their literals unchanged.
- The suite runs green in CI on a **baseline**: today's data, frozen with its origin recorded.
- The claims of the tables the simulator has not written yet are listed openly as "awaiting data", never skipped.
- The owner reads `contract.md` before 6.2 and 6.3 start.

**Two corrections to the phase plan** (made in U0, recorded in its Notes):

   - `same(full)`;
   - `derived(query)`;
   - `landing`.

   The test checks each table against the extract its `lives_in` names, on tiny's columns (D13). For `customer_changes`, it compares without `seq` and checks that the order by `seq` is the same.
2. **Step 6.1's "skipped by data folder"** is replaced by S6's wording.

## 3. What `contract.py` holds, per table

| field | example (`daily_sales`) |
|---|---|
| `name`, `tier` | `daily_sales`, 3 |
| `system` | derived from the app's order lines |
| `grain` | one row per day and product with a price that day, 1 Oct 2024 – 30 Sep 2026 |
| `key` | `sale_date, product_id` |
| `columns` | `sale_date DATE, product_id VARCHAR, qty INTEGER, amount DECIMAL(7,2)` |
| `tiny` (cut) | none (not in tiny) |
| `lives_in`, `extracts` | `full` |
| `since` | `baseline` |
| `definition` | the web shop's order lines to private customers, at list price (D7, D15) |
| `lessons` | `statistics/correlation`: walk-ins do not cause these sales; `dax` time intelligence: … (from U3a) |

The well-formed test checks:
- the key is a subset of the columns;
- a table has at most 6 columns;
- every type is from a closed set;
- `since` is `baseline` or `1.0`;
- every table has a definition.

## 5. The new tables (a draft for the owner to revise in `contract.md`)

Unless noted, each is `since='1.0'` and made by the app.

| table | key | columns (at most 6) | claims |
|---|---|---|---|
| `subscription_events` | event_id | event_id, subscription_id, event_type, reason, event_at | type in skip, pause, resume, cancel; every ended `subscribers` row has one cancel on its `ended` day; reason never null |
| `subscription_shipments` | shipment_id | shipment_id, subscription_id, product_id, shipped_on, qty | at most one per subscription and month; none in a skipped or paused month; October 2026's P-10/P-20 bags ship before 5 October (§4.2) |
| `stock_movements` | movement_id | movement_id, product_id, moved_at, reason, qty (signed) | reason in roasted, sold, shipped, written_off, counted; the balance at close 5–9 October equals `latest_stock` for P-10/P-20 (42 and 5 on 5 October); never negative |
| `green_purchases` | purchase_id | purchase_id, bought_on, origin, kg, nok_per_kg | kg above 0; kilos bought at least kilos roasted |
| `product_costs` | product_id, valid_from | product_id, valid_from, valid_to, green_nok, packaging_nok, roasting_nok | half-open periods, no overlap; cost below list price in every `prices` period |
| `shipments` | shipment_id | shipment_id, order_id, shipped_on, promised_by, delivered_on | delivered on or after shipped; `deliveries` is derived from it (S8), order 297 = 21 days |
| `pricing_rules` | rule_id | rule_id, rule_kind, percent, valid_from, valid_to | each campaign's discount is one rule on its days; at most one subscriber rule active on any day |
| `customers.kind` | a column | consumer or business | the 10-bag and 48-bag `order_values` customers are business |
| `weather_days`, `door_counts`, `till_day_totals` | shop_date | one series each (rain_mm, walk_ins, receipts) | made by the simulator as outside systems; `shop_days` equals their join |
| the country drop | parameters | day and size (phase plan §9) | Finland's orders in that week below half the mean of the four weeks around it |

**Definition claims**, one per §4.1 table of the phase plan, recompute the table from the
world's tables on `full`. For example, `daily_sales` equals the web consumer lines at list
price, cell for cell. They are the guard D15 asked for.
