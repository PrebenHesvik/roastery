## 1. Context


6.3 built the shop app and a candidate contract B (`packages/shop/tests/contract_b.txt`). 6.4
proves it on the one world whose every row is known: the teaching week (5-9 October 2026) and
C-01's year. `roastery_sim` wires the app twice over one store (C7), plays the script through
the services, plays the outside systems itself, adds the faults after a correct app (D10), cuts
and writes `tiny` in `dataset.sql`'s format, and the gate holds it cell for cell against
`packages/spec/src/roastery_spec/baseline/dataset.sql`. Then contract B is frozen (tag
`shop 0.1.0` on the owner's word). The 15 commits of 6.3 are not pushed (`origin/main` is at
6.2's close); nothing is pushed without the owner's word.

Three findings from the design, each with its fix:
1. **E-08 cannot be refused at 8 Oct 11:00**: it is written at 12:00. A relay run on 8 Oct
   22:00 forms E-08's batch alone and is refused; without it the 9 Oct 19:45 run would batch
   E-08 with E-13/E-14 and all three would wait to 10 October (W4).
2. **O-106 needs no planted fault**: `Checkout.cancel` marks L-10 deleted and `order_lines`
   is the live lines. Plan §4.2's "plants O-106" is met by the app (W19, a Note in plan.md).
3. **The simulator cannot read the app's tables back through contract B** (no reader for
   campaigns, product details, shipments, all counts, all extracts). One job is added to
   contract B (W2).
